Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

The short version: your photos stay on your phone. Donelet has no account, no server of its own and no cloud sync.

What stays on your phone

Every photo you take in Donelet, your tasks, your journal and your stickers are stored only inside the app on your device. Photos are checked on the device itself. They are never uploaded, never sent to a cloud AI and never used to train anything.

What leaves your phone

  • Usage numbers (Google Firebase Analytics): which screens are opened and simple counts such as "a task was completed". Only numbers and fixed categories — never photos, task names you type, nicknames, file paths or network addresses.
  • Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics): technical details when the app crashes, so we can fix it.
  • App settings (Google Firebase Remote Config): the app downloads settings such as feature switches. Nothing about you is sent beyond what the service needs to deliver them.
  • Purchases (Apple App Store / Google Play, Qonversion): if you buy Donelet Plus or a sticker pack, the store and Qonversion process the purchase. We never see your payment details.
  • Rewarded videos (Google AdMob): only if you choose to watch one. Donelet shows no banners and no pop-up ads.

Doing it together

"Do it together" works only on your local Wi-Fi. Your phone shares a nickname and progress numbers with nearby players for that one session. Photos are never sent to other phones.

Deleting your data

Settings › Data › Delete everything removes all photos, tasks, stickers and settings from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes them.

Children

Donelet is not directed at children under 13.
