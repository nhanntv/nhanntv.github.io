Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective: 2 November 2026 · Version 2

The short version: your photos stay on your phone. Donelet has no account and no server of its own. A few services we use receive limited technical data — usage stats and crash reports (which you can switch off), rewarded videos, purchases and app settings. Each one is listed below.

1. Who we are

Donelet is made by Trịnh Duy Nhân (NTV Tech), an individual developer ("we", "us"). We are responsible (the "controller") for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at nhanntv113@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no cloud sync. We don't run a server that stores your data.
  • Your photos, tasks, journal and stickers are kept only inside the app on your device. Photos are checked on the device and are never uploaded or used to train anything.
  • Usage stats and crash reports help us fix Donelet. You can turn them off at any time. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland they stay off until you turn them on.
  • Rewarded videos play only when you tap. On iPhone, ads are never personalised.
  • We never see your payment details and we don't sell your personal data for money.

3. What stays on your device

  • Photos. Every photo you take in Donelet is stored inside the app on your device. The photo check runs on the device, using models built into the app and, on iPhone, Apple's Vision framework, which also runs on the device. Your photos are never uploaded, never sent to a cloud AI, never seen by us and never used to train any model.
  • Tasks, journal, stickers and settings. Stored only in the app on your device. We have no copy.
  • Motion sensors. During a session, Donelet reads your phone's motion sensors to notice when the phone has been still for a while or is lying face down. The readings are used in the moment and are not stored or sent anywhere. Donelet doesn't count steps or track your activity.
  • Search, widgets and notifications. Task names may appear in your phone's search (Spotlight on iPhone), Shortcuts, home-screen widgets, the Lock Screen (Live Activities on iPhone) and reminder notifications. Your phone creates these on the device. Reminders are local notifications and don't use the internet.
  • Your phone's own backup. Donelet doesn't back up your data. If you have turned on your phone's backup (for example iCloud Backup or Android backup), your phone may include Donelet's data in your personal backup under Apple's or Google's terms. We can't access it.
  • Things you choose to send. If you save a card to your photo library, share a card or export your data (ZIP or PDF), it goes only where you send it. Donelet only adds to your photo library; it never reads it.

4. What leaves your device

Donelet uses a small number of services from other companies. Each entry below says what is sent, why, how you control it and how long it is kept; section 7 gives the legal basis. None of them receives your photos, your journal or the names you give your own tasks.

a) Usage stats and crash reports — Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics

  • What: events about how the app is used, recorded only as fixed categories and numbers (for example "a task was completed", the kind of proof used, a score from 0 to 100); which screens are opened; an app-instance ID and a Firebase installation ID (random identifiers for this installation); device model, operating system, app version and language; approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address); purchase events reported automatically by the App Store or Google Play (product and price, never payment details); and, if the app crashes, a crash report with technical details such as the stack trace and the state of the device at that moment. Never photos, task names you type, display names or file paths.
  • Why: to see which parts of Donelet work, and to find and fix bugs and crashes.
  • Your control: a separate switch, "Usage stats & crash reports", on the Terms screen when you first open the app and in Settings › Data. It is on by default, except in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, where it is off until you turn it on. To tell which region applies, the app asks Google's consent tool when it starts (see c); if the region can't be determined, the switch stays off for that session. You can turn it off at any time: collection stops and crash reports not yet sent are deleted from your phone. On a new installation nothing is collected before you make this choice: collection starts only when you continue from that first Terms screen, where the switch is shown on (or off in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). After Settings › Data › Delete everything, collection stays off until you make the choice again.
  • Kept for: crash reports, 90 days. Analytics event data, for the retention period set in our Firebase settings, after which Google deletes it; aggregated reports that don't identify you may be kept longer.
  • Google processes this data for us as our service provider. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

b) App settings — Google Firebase Remote Config

  • What: when the app starts, it downloads settings such as feature switches and limits. To do this it sends the Firebase installation ID, app version, platform, device language and time zone, and your IP address.
  • Why: to adjust the app without a new release, for example to switch off a feature that misbehaves.
  • Your control: this is part of how the app works. Without internet, the app uses its built-in settings.
  • Kept for: according to Google's retention for Firebase services.

c) Rewarded videos — Google AdMob and Google's consent tool (UMP)

  • When: unless you have Donelet Plus, the app starts Google's ad SDK when it opens so that a video can be ready in advance. The SDK connects to Google and may load a video in the background, but a video plays only when you tap to watch one. Donelet shows no banners, no pop-up ads and no ads when the app opens. Google's consent tool is also contacted at start-up, including with Plus, to check whether consent rules apply where you are and to show Google's consent form where the law requires it.
  • What Google receives: device identifiers (on iPhone the identifier for vendor, IDFV; on Android the Android advertising ID), IP address and approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions (for example whether a video was loaded, watched or completed) and diagnostics.
  • Personalisation: on iPhone, ads are never personalised and Donelet never asks to track you across other companies' apps and websites. On Android, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form decides, and you can change your choice later in Settings › Ad privacy choices; elsewhere Google may personalise ads unless you opt out in your device's ad settings (for example by deleting or resetting your advertising ID). If the App Store or Google Play tells the app you are under 18, ads are never personalised. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.
  • Your control: don't tap a video; buy Donelet Plus (the ad SDK is then not started); use the choices above.
  • Google uses this data as an independent controller under its own policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Google decides how long it keeps it.

d) Purchases — App Store, Google Play and Qonversion

  • Payment: Apple or Google processes your payment. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Qonversion is our purchase-management provider. When the app starts it connects to Qonversion with a random install ID, so that it can check and restore what you have bought. On iPhone this ID is created at random and kept in your iPhone's Keychain; it may sync through iCloud Keychain and survive reinstalling the app. On Android it is derived from a one-way hash of the device's Android ID for this app; the raw ID never leaves your phone. Qonversion also receives purchase records from the store (product, price, currency, dates, transaction IDs, renewal status) and basic device and app information.
  • Why: to unlock what you bought, restore it after reinstalling or on another device, and prevent fraud.
  • Kept for: as long as needed to provide and restore your purchases, and as long as accounting or other laws require.

e) "News from Donelet" — Firebase Cloud Messaging

  • What: off unless you turn it on in Settings › Notifications. When on, your phone receives a push token from Google and subscribes to the topics "news" and "news_<your app language>", so we can send occasional news in your language. Messages go to everyone on a topic; we don't know who you are.
  • Your control: turning it off unsubscribes you and deletes the token.

f) Age range signal — Apple Declared Age Range and Google Play Age Signals

  • Where available, the app asks the App Store or Google Play whether you are an adult. On iPhone, iOS may ask whether you want to share your age range; you can decline. The answer is used only to turn off ad personalisation for people under 18. It never blocks the app, and it is not stored or sent to us or to analytics.

g) Legal pages — GitHub Pages

  • When you open the Terms or this policy in the app, the latest version is loaded from our pages on GitHub Pages (nhanntv.github.io). Like any website, GitHub sees your IP address and basic device information. If the page can't load, the app shows the copy built into it. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

h) Emails to us

  • If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you. We keep the conversation only as long as needed to handle your request.

5. Do it together on local Wi-Fi

  • "Do it together" works only between phones on the same local network. Nothing goes through the internet and nothing reaches us.
  • While a room is open, the host's phone announces the room on the local network with its room code and the host's display name, plus the local address needed to connect (also shown as a QR code). Anyone on the same network can see this.
  • Phones in the room exchange: display name (up to 12 characters), a random player ID that changes every session, the room code, the library task each person chose (names of your own tasks are never sent), progress, score and finish time.
  • These local messages are not encrypted, so use the feature only on networks you trust. Photos are never sent to other phones.

6. Permissions

Every permission is optional. If you say no, only the related feature stops working. You can change them any time in your phone's settings.

  • Camera: to take proof photos and scan a room's QR code.
  • Motion & Fitness (iPhone): to notice when the phone is still or face down during a session.
  • Local network: to find and join nearby phones for "Do it together".
  • Notifications: for reminders and, if you turn it on, News from Donelet.
  • Photos, add only: to save a card to your photo library. Donelet never reads your photos.

Donelet never records audio and never asks for your precise location or contacts.

Where the law requires, these transfers are protected by the providers' safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK addendum) or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified.

9. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it, use Delete everything or uninstall the app.
  • Crash reports: 90 days. Analytics: as set in our Firebase settings (see 4a).
  • App settings, legal pages and rewarded videos: according to Google's or GitHub's own retention.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for your purchases and as the law requires.
  • News push token: until you turn News from Donelet off.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle your request.
  • "Do it together": nothing is kept by us.

10. Your choices and rights

In the app:

  • Settings › Data › Usage stats & crash reports — on or off at any time.
  • Settings › Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it) and your device's ad settings.
  • Settings › Notifications › News from Donelet.
  • Settings › Manage subscription — opens your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
  • Settings › Data › Delete everything — deletes all photos, tasks, journal entries, stickers and settings from the app on this phone, turns News from Donelet off, resets Firebase Analytics data and the Firebase installation ID, and deletes crash reports not yet sent. It does not reset the purchase install ID, because that ID is what lets you restore your purchases, and it can't remove data a provider already received.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone, except the iPhone Keychain install ID and anything in your own phone backups.

Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority. Because Donelet has no accounts, we can't look you up by name or email. The Support ID shown in Settings › About lets us find the purchase records that Qonversion holds for your installation. Usage stats and crash reports are not linked to the Support ID: you can switch them off at any time, and Settings › Data › Delete everything resets them on your phone and deletes the Firebase installation ID. To make a request, email nhanntv113@gmail.com with your Support ID. We may ask for information to confirm the request is yours. We reply within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires; where the law allows an extension, we'll tell you. Requests are free.

Data on your device is protected by your phone's app sandbox and screen lock. Connections to our providers use HTTPS. "Do it together" messages on the local network are not encrypted. No method of protection is perfect, so keep your phone updated and locked.
