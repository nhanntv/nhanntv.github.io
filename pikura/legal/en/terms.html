2. Description of Service

Pikura is a creative drawing and coloring application for children and adults. It has three profiles, chosen during setup and changeable later in the Parent Zone: Mini (ages 3-5), Junior (ages 6-11) and Adult. Junior and Adult use the full set of tools. The Adult profile skips the parental gate and receives ads as described in section 5; choosing it requires passing the parental gate once, so a child cannot select it alone. The App provides drawing tools, coloring pages, guided drawing lessons, and a photo-to-coloring feature. Photos used by the photo-to-coloring feature are processed entirely on the device and are never uploaded. Parents or guardians should supervise young children when they use the App.

3. Privacy

No account is required, and we do not collect names, email addresses or photos.

Drawings, photos, preferences and progress are stored only on the device and are not sent to our servers. The App does use a few third-party services, including Google AdMob for optional ads, Google Firebase and Qonversion, which receive technical data such as the IP address and device information. Our Privacy Policy lists these services and the data they receive, and its section 6 (Parents and Children) explains the choices available to parents.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional premium subscriptions ("Pikura Plus") that provide access to additional content and features. In the child profiles (Mini and Junior), all purchases require parental verification through a parental gate; the Adult profile skips the gate. Subscriptions are purchased, billed, renewed and canceled through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store under that store's terms, and refunds are handled by the store.

5. Advertising

The free version of the App shows only optional rewarded video ads, provided by Google AdMob. An ad plays only when a user chooses to watch one, for example to unlock one extra photo-to-coloring use for the day. The App does not show banner ads or interstitial (full-screen pop-up) ads, and ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested. Depending on the choices made there and on local law, Google may use data such as the IP address and device information to show and personalize ads, as described in our Privacy Policy. Pikura Plus subscribers see no ads. In the child profiles (Mini and Junior), no consent form is shown, ad requests are tagged for child-directed treatment and as coming from users under the age of consent, and ads are never personalized; the consent form and possible personalization described above apply only to the Adult profile. On iOS, ads are not personalized in any profile.
