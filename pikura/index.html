Pikura

Draw, Color & Create!

Pikura is a fun and educational drawing & coloring app designed for kids. With guided drawing lessons, hundreds of coloring pages, and free-draw mode, children can explore their creativity while developing fine motor skills.

Features

Everything for young artists

Pikura combines learning and play to help children develop artistic skills naturally.

✏️

Step-by-Step Drawing

Guided lessons teach kids to draw animals, characters, vehicles and more — stroke by stroke.

🎨

Coloring Pages

Hundreds of beautiful coloring templates across themes like animals, nature, fantasy, and holidays.

🖌️

Free Drawing Canvas

A blank canvas with brushes, stamps, stickers, and colors for unlimited creative expression.

🌈

Magic Color Effects

Neon, glitter, rainbow, and gradient brushes that make artwork sparkle and shine.

📸

Save & Share

Save creations to a personal gallery and share with family and friends.

🔒

Safe for Kids

No ads, no in-app purchases for kids, parental controls, and fully COPPA-compliant.
