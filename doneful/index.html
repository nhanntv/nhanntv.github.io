How it works

Three kinds of proof

📷

One photo

A glass of water, a book, a plant. Click looks for that thing in the shot.

🔁

Before and after

The desk, a shelf, the kitchen counter, the bed, the TV. Click compares the two shots and shows how much changed.

✋

Your word

One photo for the journal, no checking.

Click can be wrong, so “I did it” is always there. The photo check never holds back a sticker.

Got it. One down.

Meet Click

A sticker for every task

Click, a little instant camera with legs, looks at the photo on the spot and prints you a sticker for your weekly journal.

Every finished task becomes a print with a sticker, and each task has its own set of six. A new journal page starts every Monday.

The clock only counts up. Breaks are fine.

Private by design

Photos never leave your phone

  • ✓No account and no sign-up.
  • ✓Photos are checked on your phone. Doneful never uploads them; they leave your phone only when you share them yourself.
  • ✓Doing it together sends only progress and scores between phones, never photos.
  • ✓Doneful counts anonymous app usage and crash reports to fix bugs. It never collects your photos or the task names you type.
