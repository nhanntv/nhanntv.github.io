Privacy Policy

Effective from: July 2026 · Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sentinel (the "App", "we", "us", "our") handles information. Sentinel is an offline-first, privacy-first tool that helps you scan your surroundings for hidden cameras and covert recording devices. Your scans stay on your device. We designed Sentinel so that, by default, no scan data ever leaves your phone.

1. What Sentinel Is — and What It Is Not

Sentinel is an awareness and triage aid, not a guaranteed detector. It uses your phone's existing sensors to surface possible signs of surveillance devices. It cannot find every hidden device, and it can never confirm that a space is "100% safe." Results may include both false alarms and missed devices. Always combine Sentinel with a careful manual inspection, and seek professional help when your safety may be at risk.

2. Our Privacy Principles

  • Offline-first: Scanning works without an internet connection. Sensor readings and detections are processed on your device.
  • No account required: You can use the core scanning features without signing up or providing personal information.
  • Your scans stay local: Scan results, detected devices, and history are stored only on your device. We do not upload them.
  • Minimal data: The only data that may leave your device is anonymized diagnostics (which you can turn off) and purchase validation (handled by the app stores).

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Scan & Sensor Data (On-Device Only)

  • Sensor readings: Wi-Fi network scan results, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) advertisements, camera frames (for lens-glint and infrared viewing), magnetometer/motion readings, and Time-of-Flight/LiDAR depth data, depending on which scans you run and what your device supports.
  • Detections & history: Suspicious-device records, risk scores, scan sessions, and notes you save.
  • This data is created and stored locally on your device in an on-device database. Camera frames are processed in real time for analysis and are not saved as part of normal scanning. We do not transmit any of this data off your device by default.

3.2 Diagnostics & Crash Data (Optional, Opt-Out)

  • Crash reports & analytics: To keep the App stable, we may collect anonymized crash logs, error information, device type, operating system version, app version, and basic feature-usage events through Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics.
  • This information is not linked to your identity and never includes your scan results, detected devices, camera images, or the contents of any space you inspect.
  • You can turn diagnostics off at any time in Settings.

3.3 Subscription Data

  • If you purchase a Pro subscription, the relevant app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and our subscription manager Qonversion process the transaction and tell the App whether your subscription is active.
  • We do not receive or store your full payment-card details. Billing is handled entirely by the app stores.

3.4 What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, contacts, photos library, precise location history, or browsing activity.
  • We do not upload your scan results, detected-device lists, or camera/sensor data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to anyone.

4. Device Permissions and Why We Need Them

Sentinel asks for permissions contextually — one at a time, with a short explanation, only when a feature needs it. You may decline any permission; the related scan simply will not be available. Each permission is used solely for on-device scanning.

  • Camera: Used for the lens-glint optical scan (detecting reflections from camera lenses with the torch) and the infrared viewer. Camera frames are analyzed on-device in real time and are not stored.
  • Location (precise) & nearby Wi-Fi: On Android, the operating system requires location permission in order to perform Wi-Fi network scans. We use it only to enumerate nearby Wi-Fi networks for the Wi-Fi scan. We do not track, store, or transmit your geographic location.
  • Bluetooth / nearby devices: Used to scan for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices that may be cameras, trackers, or recorders.
  • Motion & sensors (magnetometer): Used for the magnetometer scan, which looks for magnetic-field anomalies near electronics. This is a low-confidence, "reference only" signal.

5. How We Use Information

  • To run scans: Sensor data is analyzed on your device to highlight possible surveillance devices and produce a risk score.
  • To show history: Your detections and scan sessions are saved locally so you can review them.
  • To improve stability: Anonymized diagnostics (if enabled) help us find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To manage your subscription: Purchase status from the stores/Qonversion unlocks Pro features.

6. On-Device Data Handling and Security

  • Local storage: Scans, detections, and history are stored in a database on your device. Deleting the App removes this data.
  • No background uploads: Scan data is not synced to any server.
  • Encryption in transit: The limited data that does leave your device (diagnostics, purchase validation) is sent over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS).
  • No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for the physical security of your device.

7. Optional: Community Surveillance-Device Database

We may offer an optional, opt-in feature that lets you contribute anonymized fingerprints of suspicious devices to help build a community database that improves detection for everyone.

  • This feature is OFF by default. It is separate from accepting the Terms of Service — accepting the Terms does not enable sharing.
  • If you opt in, only anonymized technical signal traits may be shared — for example, an OUI/vendor prefix or generic signal characteristics of a suspicious device.
  • We do NOT share any personal data, camera images or other media, the contents of the space you scanned, or your geographic location.
  • You can turn this off and revoke sharing at any time in Settings.

8. Diagnostics and Analytics (Firebase)

  • We use Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting) and Firebase Analytics (anonymous usage metrics) to improve reliability and understand which features are used.
  • Collected data may include device model, OS version, app version, crash stack traces, and anonymous event counts. It is not used to identify you and contains no scan content.
  • You can opt out at any time in Settings; diagnostics may be enabled by default and can be disabled.

9. Subscriptions and Purchases

  • Sentinel offers an optional Pro subscription that unlocks advanced scanning modules.
  • Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store and managed via Qonversion for cross-platform entitlement tracking.
  • Billing information is handled by the app stores under their own privacy policies. We receive only your subscription status.

10. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal information, and we do not share your scan data with third parties.

We share only the limited categories of data described above, and only with the service providers needed to deliver the App:

  • Firebase (Google): Anonymized crash reporting and analytics (opt-out).
  • Apple App Store / Google Play / Qonversion: Subscription billing and status.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety. Because scan data stays on your device, it is not available to us to disclose.

11. Children's Privacy

  • Sentinel is not directed at children and is intended for adults and older teens (at least 13, or 16 where required by local law).
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the applicable age. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will address it.

12. Data Retention

  • Scan data & history: Kept on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. You can clear history in the App at any time.
  • Diagnostics: Crash and analytics data are retained by Firebase for a limited period (typically up to 12–14 months) and then automatically deleted or anonymized.
  • Subscription data: Retained by the app stores and Qonversion according to their policies.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA in California).

  • Because your scan data lives only on your device, you can exercise most rights directly — by viewing, exporting where available, or deleting your data and uninstalling the App.
  • For diagnostics or subscription data, contact us at the email below and we will respond within the time required by your local law (generally within 30 days).
  • California residents: we do not sell or "share" personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.
