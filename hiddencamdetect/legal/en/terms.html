2. Description of Service

Sentinel uses your device's existing sensors to surface possible signs of surveillance devices. Depending on your device's capabilities, it may include:

  • Wi-Fi network scan — lists nearby Wi-Fi devices and flags patterns that may indicate cameras.
  • Lens-glint optical scan — uses the camera and torch to spot reflections from lenses.
  • Bluetooth (BLE) scan — detects nearby Bluetooth devices.
  • Magnetometer scan — a low-confidence, "reference only" check for magnetic anomalies.
  • Infrared (IR) viewer — helps you look for infrared LEDs used by some cameras.
  • Time-of-Flight / LiDAR scan — depth-based inspection on supported devices.
  • Guided manual checklist — step-by-step instructions to inspect a room by hand.
