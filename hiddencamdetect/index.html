Detection Toolkit

Many sensors, one honest verdict

Hidden Cam Detect combines several detection methods and a weighted risk score — no single trick, no false confidence.

📡

Wi-Fi Network Scan

Sweep the local network for IP cameras and hidden devices — each identified by type (camera, doorbell, NVR, TV box, router and more).

📷

Camera Lens Detection

Your torch and camera reveal the tell-tale retro-reflective glint of a concealed lens behind mirrors, smoke detectors or vents.

🔵

Bluetooth Scan

Surface nearby Bluetooth recorders, trackers and covert gadgets, resolved to a manufacturer where possible.

🌡️

Infrared Viewer

Spot the invisible IR LEDs that night-vision spy cameras rely on in the dark, through your front camera.

🧭

Magnetometer

A reference check for the magnetic signature of concealed electronics — clearly labelled as a supporting hint.

✅

Guided Manual Sweep

An honest, room-by-room checklist — because no tool is perfect, every scan pairs technology with your own eyes.

🛡️ Honest by design

Hidden Cam Detect is a triage & guidance aid — not a 100% guarantee. It surfaces suspicious signals with the methods your phone supports, and always recommends a manual sweep. Your scan data never leaves the device.
