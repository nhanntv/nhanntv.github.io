Why it feels different

Most tidying games race you. Roomlore does the opposite.

⏳

No timer, no score

Nothing counts down and nothing is graded. Put an object in the wrong place and it simply returns to your hand.

🗺️

Eight real places

Tokyo, Paris, Huế, the Nordics, the 80s, Taipei, Amsterdam, Kyoto. Fictional homes in neighbourhoods that exist.

📜

Objects that remember

Three objects in every room carry a memory. Put them away and you learn a little more about whoever lived here.

🔊

Sound you can feel

Wood, ceramic, fabric, metal, paper and glass each sound and feel different as they land.

✈️

Plays offline

Everything except downloading a new collection works with no connection at all.

🔒

No account, ever

Nothing to sign up for, no cloud sync, no social feed. Your progress lives on your device and nowhere else.

How it plays

Three steps, and not one word of instructions in the room itself.

1

Open the boxes

Each room arrives packed. Everything you need is in the tray at the bottom of the screen.

2

Find where it belongs

Every object has one right home. Drag it there and it settles in with a sound that fits what it is made of.

3

Read the room

When the last object is away, the room is quiet — and three of the things you just put down have something to tell you.
